景行 / AcquireEnglish reading samples
=====================================

English A1–B2 JSON files are original graded samples for the AcquireEnglish
flavor only (not loaded by Jingway builds).

Jingway ships one Chinese test sample (`cn_test_train_window.json`) for QA.
Production reading content should be user-imported TXT/EPUB/PDF — do not
bundle 部编版教材正文.
