景行 / AcquireEnglish reading samples
=====================================

English A1–B2 JSON files under this folder are original graded samples for the
AcquireEnglish flavor. They are NOT third-party novels or exam reprints.

Chinese `cn_*.json` files are original short prose written for 景行语文
(Jingway). They are NOT 部编版教材正文 and must not be replaced with
copyrighted textbook passages.

User-imported TXT/EPUB/PDF and personal materials remain the user's content.
Do not redistribute commercial books inside the APK.
